# Dracula account privacy notice — draft

This draft must not be presented as a complete privacy notice until the operator's identity and contact details, retention periods, backup deletion arrangements, hosting and Google processing details are confirmed.

## What an account uses

Google authenticates the user and gives Dracula a Google account identifier, display name and, when available, a profile picture URL. Dracula stores the identifier, display name, picture URL and account/session records in its database. Users may edit their Dracula display name. Dracula does not store the Google account password or the Google email address as part of this sign-in flow.

Dracula uses a session cookie in the browser to keep the user signed in. Sessions expire after 30 days. Expired and revoked session records are removed when a later sign-in challenge is created, so cleanup depends on sign-in activity.

## Deleting an account

The profile page offers account deletion. The active database removes the profile, linked sign-in identifier and associated sessions. This does not delete the user's Google account or revoke access in Google account settings. Deletion from backups depends on backup retention and restoration procedures, which must be confirmed.
